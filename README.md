从“去哪玩”到“带什么出发”，两份报告搞定一次旅行规划。
From where to go to what to pack — plan your trip in two reports.

# 光行旅行助手｜Guangxing Travel Skill ✈️

## 中文介绍

一款旅行规划助手，通过前后两份报告，帮你更轻松、高效地完成旅行规划。

从 **“去哪玩”**、**“怎么玩”**、**“路线怎么安排”**，到 **“出发前该准备什么”**，把零散的旅行信息一步步整理成真正可执行的旅行计划。

---

## 【报告 1】一站式个性化旅行攻略

不是泛泛而谈的通用攻略，而是根据你的真实旅行需求，生成更适合你的个性化内容。

它会综合考虑：

- **出行节奏**：松弛游 / 特种兵式旅行
- **需求偏好**：享受自然 / 看日出 / 吃美食 / 人像出片 / 无人机拍摄等
- **同行人**：朋友 / 伴侣 / 老人 / 孩子
- **特殊需求**：徒步 / 潜水 / 户外活动等

目标不是简单告诉你“这里有什么景点”，而是帮你先把更适合你的地点筛出来。

面对一堆景点时，真正回答这个问题：

**这么多地方，我到底去哪玩？**

### 报告内容包括

- **图文攻略**
  - 图片 + 文字组合展示
  - 更直观了解地点体验

- **丰富信息**
  - 建议游玩时长
  - 核心亮点
  - 门票 / 预约信息
  - 个性化推荐理由
  - 注意事项

- **地图与路线辅助**
  - 同时考虑大交通与小交通
  - 辅助判断路线、距离与通行时长

完成地点筛选后，可以 **一键复制已选地点信息**，继续进入第二次调用。

---

## 【报告 2】完整图文路线 + 行前准备 + 行李箱攻略

第二份报告会把你已经选好的地点，进一步整理成一份真正能拿来出发的完整旅行方案。

包含：

### 路线总览
- 整体行程结构
- 每日路线安排
- 大交通与小交通建议
- 时间与顺序规划

### 每日旅行规划
- 每日游玩路线
- 游玩建议
- 时间分配
- 景点之间的衔接方式

### 亮点攻略
- 穿衣建议
- 出片穿搭推荐
- 装备提醒
- 不同场景下的准备建议

### 行前准备与行李箱
- 行前准备事项
- 行李物资清单
- 支持删除、增加、编辑
- 对照清单整理行李，减少遗漏

从路线到装备，从穿搭到物资，把“攻略”进一步变成真正可以执行的旅行方案。

## English

Guangxing is an AI-powered travel planning assistant designed to help you plan trips more easily and efficiently through two structured reports.

From **where to go**, **what to do**, and **how to arrange your route**, to **what to prepare before departure**, Guangxing helps turn scattered travel ideas into a clear and actionable plan.

---

## Report 1 — Personalized Travel Guide

Instead of generating a generic travel guide, Guangxing creates personalized recommendations based on your actual travel style and needs.

It takes into account factors such as:

- **Travel pace**: relaxed travel or intensive sightseeing
- **Preferences**: nature, sunrise, food, portrait photography, drone photography, and more
- **Travel companions**: friends, couples, elderly family members, or children
- **Special interests**: hiking, diving, outdoor activities, etc.

The goal is not simply to show you a list of attractions.

It helps answer a more important question:

**Out of all these places, which ones are actually right for me?**

### The report includes

- **Visual travel guide**
  - Images combined with written recommendations
  - A more intuitive way to understand each destination

- **Detailed attraction information**
  - Recommended visit duration
  - Key highlights
  - Ticket and reservation information
  - Personalized reasons for recommendation
  - Important tips and notes

- **Map and transportation support**
  - Long-distance and local transportation
  - Route planning and estimated travel time

Once you have selected your preferred places, you can **copy the selected destination information with one click** and move on to the second report.

---

## Report 2 — Complete Itinerary, Route and Packing Guide

The second report turns your selected destinations into a complete travel plan that is ready to use.

It includes:

### Route Overview
- Overall itinerary structure
- Daily route planning
- Long-distance and local transportation
- Travel sequence and timing suggestions

### Daily Itinerary
- Daily travel routes
- Activity suggestions
- Time allocation
- Connections between destinations

### Travel Highlights
- Clothing recommendations
- Outfit suggestions for photography
- Equipment reminders
- Preparation tips for different travel scenarios

### Pre-trip Preparation & Packing List
- Pre-departure reminders
- Packing checklist
- Editable items: add, remove, or modify
- Pack directly against the checklist to reduce the chance of forgetting something

From route planning to outfits, from equipment to packing, Guangxing helps turn a travel guide into a trip you can actually follow.

## 如何使用｜How to Use

### 方法 ：直接下载 Skill 包

1. 点击仓库中的 `plan-glow-trip-v3-SkillPay-fixed.zip`
2. 点击右上角 `Download raw file` 下载到本地
3. 在支持上传 / 安装 Skill 的 AI 工具中导入该压缩包
4. 导入完成后，直接告诉 AI 你的旅行需求即可

### 推荐调用指令

请使用 **光行｜两阶段旅行规划 V3** 帮我进行两阶段旅行规划。

先执行第一阶段，询问：
- 出发地
- 目的地
- 出行时间
- 同行人
- 旅行要求
- 大交通情况

## English Usage

1. Download `plan-glow-trip-v3-SkillPay-fixed.zip`
2. Import it into an AI tool that supports custom Skills
3. Tell the AI your destination, travel dates, companions, travel pace, preferences, and special requirements
4. Generate Report 1 first, select your preferred places, then continue with Report 2

### Recommended Prompt

Please use **Guangxing | Two-Stage Travel Planning V3** to help me plan my trip in two stages.

Start with Stage 1 and ask me about:
- Departure city
- Destination
- Travel dates
- Travel companions
- Travel preferences and requirements
- Long-distance transportation arrangements

如果你当前使用的平台不支持 Skill 安装，也可以解压文件后，将其中的 SKILL.md 内容作为系统提示词 / 自定义指令使用。
